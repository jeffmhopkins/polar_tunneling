Pitch CSW% Whiff% Swing% Deception

CSW% is called strikes plus whiffs per pitch. Whiff% is per pitch, not per swing. Deception is how many CSW points come from how this pitch looks to the hitter. That covers how far it misses where a fastball would have gone, how it moves across his view at the decision point, and how hard it is to tell from the pitcher's other pitches. It's measured against the same pitch type with a league-typical look, in the same count and sequence.

How to read this The model found a real pitch-to-pitch effect. In a single pre-registered 2025 → 2026 test, the perceptual features improved whiff and swing predictions beyond stuff, location, count, the previous pitch and speed. A pitcher's average on these features did not predict his results in held-out games. So use this to choose pitches in a situation, not to rank pitchers. The previous pitch is the pitcher's average pitch of that type, at its average location. The previous result is the most common one leading into the count. The hitter is league-average and seeing the pitcher for the first time. Differences of a point or less between options are within noise. Pitchers with at least 1,000 regular-season pitches in 2026 are included. Each pitch type must be at least 4% of his mix to that side, with at least 40 pitches.
