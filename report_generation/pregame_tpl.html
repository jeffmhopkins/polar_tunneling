Where the plan breaks from habit

Most of the time the plan agrees with what he already throws. These are the counts where it doesn't, ranked by runs at stake: how much the model's pick beats his usual pitch there, times how often a plate appearance reaches that count.

These are the model's own estimates, and they run high. In the 2026 season test, throwing the model's pick instead of the pitcher's habit saved under 0.1 runs per 100 pitches, and that gap was within noise. Read the ranking as where to look, not as runs in the bank.
